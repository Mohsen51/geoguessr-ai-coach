## Identifying Iraq

- The coverage in Iraq is limited to a single trekker in the **Iraqi National Museum** in Baghdad.
- All of the coverage in Iraq is taken by a **trekker** with a camera similar to **Gen 2 **but with distinctly worse image quality and colour. This coverage tends to be overexposed, even becoming blindingly so [at some points](https://goo.gl/maps/YyzKFREKZvguinm16). NOTE: This camera quality is sometimes referred to as Gen 1.5.
- The trekker blur has a well-defined, **pointed** **shape**.
- The only piece of coverage outdoors is a small patio with a diagonal walkway. In this patio you can see many **date palms**. This is the only piece of the coverage that can rarely appear on serious maps.
- The inside of the museum features statues, steles, a variety of [artefacts](https://goo.gl/maps/WXRcTXkZSMVuChJK6), and a few [back rooms](https://goo.gl/maps/cjfVfUA3sbEWXegF6).
